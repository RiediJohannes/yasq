# Credits

YASQ includes some sample data out-of-the-box to help users get started with creating their own quiz or customizing the app.
Naturally, we only ship assets that are licensed under Creative Commons or comparable licensing terms.

Nevertheless, we think it is fair to give credit to the original creators of our sample data.
